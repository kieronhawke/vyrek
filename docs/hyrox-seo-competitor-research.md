# HYROX coaching SERP + site teardown research

Compiled 29 July 2026 from live SERP sampling and site teardowns. Caveat:
search sampling was US-geolocated; domain sets are reliable, exact UK
positions are indicative. Source URLs in the underlying reports.

## Who actually ranks for the money terms

| Query family | Who wins today | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|---|
| "hyrox coach" / "coaching uk" | Official coaches.hyrox.com directory + "become a coach" course content | Client-seeking intent is barely served; a single thin directory profile outranks whole businesses |

| HYROX news | hybridfitnessmedia.com (~538 URLs, near-daily) | News never converts to evergreen or product |

## The ranked competitor set

1. coaches.hyrox.com (brand authority; also a directory we can JOIN — an
   optimised Ben profile linking home is free top-3 presence)
2. Rox Lyfe — closest analogue: UK, coaching + content + podcast; hides
   pricing, publishes no methodology, coaches are credible but not elite-
   division athletes
3. Centr — official partner app; generic plans, no humans on the page
4. Hybrid Athlete Club — £44.99/mo membership + tools + programmatic
   per-race product pages (good pattern, badly maintained: ranking 404s)
5. Jake Dearden / The Hyrox Plan — the only elite-athlete-fronted rival
   (Doubles World Champion); strong PR, weak owned SEO
6. PureGym blog — blocks "training plan" with a free 12-week page
7. Box Nutrition — science/testing angle, £12-249 products, no elite front
8. HWPO / HAOS — US elite-fronted programme brands
9. London gym cluster — Third Space, Gymbox, BXR, Apex ("PhD-level
   experts", no names, no proof)
10. Commodity plan sellers (Hyrox Workouts by Code, TrainingPeaks)

Marchon deserves special note: massive brand, £149 one-off HYROX block,
supplements and apparel — and effectively ZERO SEO surface. Their
audience is interceptable mid-funnel with content.

## What we beat them on (the honest scorecard)

1. **A named, verifiable elite athlete.** Almost every ranking coaching
   page hides its coaches. Ben's record (dossier: 48:35 doubles PB, 6th
   at Worlds Elite 15) with Person/Athlete schema and press links is an
   E-E-A-T weapon only Dearden can match, and his site is weak.
2. **Personalisation as published methodology.** Every rival ships
   templates or hides how coaching works. We publish the intake → plan →
   Sunday-recalibration process with a sample week.
3. **"Couch to Hyrox" ownership.** Definitive free guide + email course
   into the funnel; the current SERP is embarrassingly weak.
4. **Free-plan content that out-does PureGym** (videos, benchmarks,
   downloads) funnelling to a consultation instead of a gym membership.
5. **Per-event race pages done properly** (HAC's pattern, maintained) —
   dates, standards, taper timing, countdown-matched plans.
6. **City coaching pages that sell coaching** where gyms sell classes —
   our 130 geo pages already exist; enrich with real local data.
7. **Free consultation with the athlete himself** — nobody in the entire
   set offers a call with an Elite 15 athlete as the entry point.
8. **Tools tied to a human funnel** (plan-maker PDF → account → call),
   versus orphaned calculators.
9. **Ecosystem entries**: list Ben on coaches.hyrox.com; pursue official
   HYROX Online Training Partner status (Rox Lyfe/Centr hold it today).
10. **Price positioning**: the market hides prices at the top and
    commoditises at the bottom; our tailored-on-a-free-call model
    undercuts local PT economics without printing a number.

Note on price transparency: the SERP research argued published prices
convert better (nobody in coaching publishes them). Kieron's decision is
no public pricing for now — revisit with real funnel data once
consultations are flowing.

## Mistakes to avoid (observed failures)

- Thin programmatic at hyroxvault scale (2,871 swap-the-town pages)
- Zero SEO surface (Marchon, RoxFit) — renting discovery from social
- Stale programmatic (HAC's ranking 404s)
- Application-gated everything with no methodology shown (Rox Lyfe)
- JS-only tool pages that fetch as empty shells (RoxHype)
- "hyrox" in the domain (trademark exposure; hyroxvault is the outlier)
