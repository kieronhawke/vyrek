# Suth Performance — Offer & Delivery Plan

Prepared 29 July 2026. Builds on docs/growth-plan.md, the competitor
pricing research, and the HYROX SERP research. Policy baked in
throughout: **no pricing is published on the site** — every path leads
to a free consultation with Ben, and price bands below are INTERNAL
guidance for the call. All bands are proposals until Kieron/Ben sign
them off (decision list in section 9).

---

## 1. The offer architecture

One ladder, two entry doors (beginner door and HYROX door), everything
converging on the free consultation.

```
FREE LAYER (capture)
  Plan-maker PDF · Couch-to-Hyrox email course · tools · guides
        │
        ▼
FREE CONSULTATION WITH BEN  ←——— every path leads here
        │
        ├── SELF-SERVE  ──  T1 The Hub
        ├── COACHED     ──  T2 Hub Plus · T3 Online Coaching
        ├── PREMIUM     ──  T4 Elite 1:1 (application)
        └── ONE-OFF     ──  T1.5 12-Week Race Block · corporate
```

The ladder's logic: capture cheaply at the bottom, coach profitably in
the middle, anchor with scarcity at the top. Every tier names its
upgrade path inside the product.

## 2. Offer spec sheets

### T0 — Free account (the capture layer)

- **Who**: everyone who isn't ready to talk yet.
- **Promise**: "A real plan, free, in three minutes."
- **Inside**: the HYROX plan-maker (branded PDF), pace tools, saved quiz
  results, a weekly email from Ben (one idea, one session, one nudge).
- **Delivery cost**: zero marginal Ben-time. Fully automated.
- **Job**: email list + consultation pipeline. Target: every free
  account gets the consultation offer at generation, day 3, and day 10.

### T1 — The Hub (self-serve subscription)

- **Who**: self-starters; price-sensitive; the "hands-off" customers
  Kieron described who want content and structure, not conversations.
- **Promise**: "Elite structure without the hand-holding."
- **Inside**: personalised dated 12-week programme (quiz-driven), Sunday
  recalibration from logs, full station video library, Ben's coaching
  hub content (courses: First Race series, station masterclasses,
  beginner foundations), community Q&A where Ben answers asynchronously.
- **Delivery cost**: Ben's content filming (front-loaded, reusable) plus
  ~1-2h/week answering community questions at scale.
- **Internal band**: £8.99/mo (existing billing) or £79/yr. The
  competitor set's paid apps run £16-50/mo — we are deliberately the
  cheapest credible option to capture the low tier.
- **Close path**: self-serve checkout straight after the quiz; the
  consultation is offered but never required.
- **Upgrade path**: "Ben reviewed your last block" moments; a monthly
  in-app prompt to book the free consultation for Online Coaching.

### T1.5 — 12-Week Race Block (one-off)

- **Who**: race-booked athletes who won't subscribe (proven £55-149
  one-off market: Rox Lyfe £55, Marchon £149).
- **Promise**: "Your race, twelve weeks, one block, done properly."
- **Inside**: the personalised programme dated to their race + race-week
  protocol + pacing plan; includes Hub access for the 12 weeks.
- **Internal band**: £79-99 one-off, positioned on the call when
  someone resists a subscription.
- **Delivery cost**: none beyond the Hub's automation.

### T2 — Hub Plus (coached-lite subscription)

- **Who**: wants Ben's presence without full coaching; the bridge tier.
- **Promise**: "The Hub, with Ben checking in."
- **Inside**: everything in T1 + monthly live group Q&A with Ben +
  priority answers + one form-check video review per month + quarterly
  plan review (async, written by Ben).
- **Delivery cost**: 1 group call/month (1h serves everyone), form
  checks ~10 min each, quarterly reviews ~15 min each. At 100 members:
  roughly 4-6h/week. Scales sub-linearly (the group call is fixed cost).
- **Internal band**: £24.99/mo. Launch with founding pricing (RoxFit
  Ultra pattern): first cohort locks a lower rate for life.
- **Close path**: consultation or self-serve.

### T3 — Online Coaching (the core revenue tier)

- **Who**: the £100-150/mo client Kieron specified. Serious beginners
  who want accountability; HYROX athletes chasing a time.
- **Promise**: "Ben in your corner, every week."
- **Inside**: programme written personally by Ben (not generated),
  weekly review + adjustments from logs, private messaging (defined
  response window: within 24h Mon-Fri), monthly 30-min 1:1 video call,
  race-day strategy, off-plan life support (holidays, injuries, work
  chaos absorbed into the plan).
- **Delivery cost per client**: ~2h/month (weekly reviews ~20 min ×4 +
  monthly call 30 min + messaging). 30 clients ≈ 14h/week. 50 clients ≈
  23h/week — likely past Ben's ceiling alongside 12-14h of his own
  training. **Capacity question (section 9) gates this tier's size.**
- **Internal band**: £119-149/mo, 3-month initial commitment,
  commitment-length discount available as a call-close tool (the
  Ultimate Performance pattern) when the diary is full.
- **Close path**: ALWAYS via the consultation. Ben qualifies, picks the
  band, sends the payment link from admin.
- **Upgrade path**: Elite when a client's goals outgrow the format.

### T4 — Elite 1:1 (the anchor)

- **Who**: competitive athletes, Elite ambitions, high earners who want
  the full programme.
- **Promise**: "Train with Ben the way Ben trains."
- **Inside**: everything in T3, weekly calls instead of monthly, race
  selection and season planning, race-week and race-day support,
  priority everything.
- **Delivery cost per client**: ~6h/month. Hard cap proposed: 6-10
  places. Show the true number of open places on /coaching — scarcity
  must be real (hard rule: no fabricated urgency).
- **Internal band**: £299-399/mo, application only.
- **Job beyond revenue**: makes T3 look reasonably priced, and produces
  the flagship case studies (public race results = verifiable proof).

### B2B — Corporate & partner (later, evidenced demand)

`corporate personal training` (170/mo, KD 10, $3.07 CPC) has nobody
competing. Offer: workplace HYROX/fitness programmes (group Hub access
+ monthly virtual session + a challenge leaderboard). Custom-quoted on
a call. Build only after T1-T3 run smoothly.

## 3. The free layer, itemised

Each magnet exists to start the email relationship and book calls:

1. **HYROX plan-maker** (build first): short quiz → genuinely useful
   12-week outline → branded PDF → account + email sequence. Beats the
   saturated calculator SERP by being personal, not arithmetical.
2. **Couch to Hyrox** email course (near-vacant SERP, flagship pillar):
   30 days, one email a day, soft beginner tone.
3. **Free 12-week plans per level** as long-form pages (out-do
   PureGym's 3,000-worder), each funnelling to the consultation.
4. **Race-day checklist + pacing chart PDFs** (race-city page magnets).
5. **Weekly email from Ben** — one idea, one session, one nudge; the
   compounding asset.

## 4. The consultation (how the close works)

15-20 minutes, video or phone. The call is a fitness assessment first
and a sale second — that ordering is the brand.

Skeleton:
1. **Where are you now** (2-3 min): training history, injuries, life.
2. **Where do you want to be** (3-5 min): the goal behind the goal
   (beginner: confidence/health; athlete: the number).
3. **The honest gap** (3-5 min): what it takes per week; Ben's read on
   a realistic timeline. This is the value even if they never buy.
4. **The fit** (3-5 min): which tier matches goals AND budget; quote
   from the internal band; one option, not a menu ("for you I'd start
   with…"). If they're not right for coaching, say so and give them the
   Hub or the free plan — the refusal builds the brand.
5. **Close**: payment link sent from admin while on the call; account
   provisioned on payment; first-week expectations set.

Ops: leads arrive from /free-consultation and the quiz; admin pipeline
tracks new → contacted → call booked → won/lost; wire a scheduling tool
(Cal.com) so leads self-book into Ben's defined call windows (e.g. two
90-min blocks/week = ~10-12 calls/week capacity).

## 5. Delivery operations (Ben's week)

Ben's constraint: he trains 12-14h/week and races a full season. A
sustainable coaching load around that, at steady state:

| Activity | Hours/week |
|---|---|
| Consultations (2 blocks) | 3 |
| T3 weekly reviews + messaging (30 clients) | 12-14 |
| T4 Elite (6 clients) | 8-9 |
| Hub Plus group call + form checks | 4-5 |
| Community Q&A (Hub) | 1-2 |
| Content filming (batched monthly) | 2 (averaged) |
| **Total** | **30-35** |

That is a full working week on top of elite training — the model works
but has no slack. Levers when full: raise T3 band, close T3 to a
waitlist (real scarcity copy), promote self-serve harder, or (later) a
second coach under Ben's methodology — flagged as a brand decision, not
assumed.

Revenue at that steady state (mid-band, illustrative only):
30×£129 + 6×£349 + 100×£24.99 + 400×£8.99 ≈ £12.0k/mo before the free
layer converts anyone new. The subscription base is what scales past
Ben's hours; the coached tiers are what pay for the pipeline today.

## 6. Retention and expansion

- **Race cycles are the retention engine**: every plan ends at a race;
  every race debrief (T3/T4: a call; Hub: an automated review) opens
  the next block. "What's next?" is asked while the medal is warm.
- **Renewal moments automated in admin**: week 10 of any 12-week block
  flags the client for a next-block conversation.
- **Referrals**: give a month, get a month (both sides), leaning on the
  existing partner-programme rails.
- **Pause, don't cancel**: injury/holiday pause preserves the
  subscription relationship.
- **Win-back**: 90 days after a cancel, one honest email from Ben.

## 7. Proof strategy (the missing asset)

We launch with zero testimonials by honesty policy. The plan to build
real proof fast:
1. Founding cohort offer (Hub Plus/T3): reduced founding rate in
   exchange for tracked results and permission to publish them.
2. **Verifiable results as the format**: before/after race times that
   anyone can check on public HYROX results — no competitor's
   testimonials can say that.
3. Ben's own race reports every race weekend (content + proof + entity
   building in one).
4. Case studies ship to the site only with written consent and real
   names/times — never composites.

## 9. Decisions needed (do not assume)

1. **Price bands** per tier (section 2) — sign-off or adjustment.
2. **Ben's true weekly coaching capacity** — gates T3/T4 sizes and the
   consultation call windows.
3. **Commitment terms** — 3-month minimum on T3? Rolling monthly?
4. **Founding pricing** mechanics and how long it lasts.
5. **Second-coach question** — is "Ben's team" ever acceptable, or is
   this permanently a one-coach brand? (Affects the ceiling of T3.)
6. **Guarantee** — e.g. "if you follow the plan for 12 weeks and don't
   improve, the next block is free". Strong converter, real cost;
   needs Ben's comfort and clean terms.
7. **Corporate** — pursue now or park until T1-T3 are steady.
8. **Supabase restore + Resend domain** — both block parts of the ops
   layer today.
