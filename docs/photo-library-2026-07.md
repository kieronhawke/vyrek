# Own-photography library — July 2026 intake

**Added:** 30 July 2026
**Source:** `~/Downloads/For Hawke` (34 files) plus three added separately the same morning.
**In the repo:** `public/media/images/camp/` (19) and `public/media/images/race/` (18).
**Originals:** left in `~/Downloads`, 4.2 GB. Not copied into the repo. The
19 camp frames are 3376×6000 PNGs at roughly 90 MB each, so re-crops should
be taken from there rather than from the sized copies.

Sized with sharp/mozjpeg: long edge 2200 px, quality 72, 4:4:4, metadata
stripped. 8.5 MB for the set, in line with the existing `/track` shoot
(16 MB across 47 files). The three Chicago frames arrived at 800×1200 and
were left at native size rather than upscaled.

Every frame is portrait, and every site image slot is landscape, so each one
also has a **`-wide.jpg` 16:9 variant** cut from the original at 2000 px, with
the crop position chosen by eye per frame rather than centred. Use the
portrait file in a tall or square slot (coach tile, quiz interstitial, mobile
full-bleed) and the `-wide` file everywhere else. Section 7 has the detail.

Rights, identity and usage confirmed by Kieron, 30 July 2026: the set is
cleared for use anywhere on the site, the red-bearded athlete throughout is
**Ben Sutherland**, and crops and adjustments are at our discretion.

## 7. Portrait and landscape

Every frame is portrait, 2:3 or 9:16. The site's image slots are landscape:
blog hero `16/9` (`4/3` at `lg`), post cards `16/10` and `16/9`, MDX inline
`aspect-video`, station guide `16/9`. `object-cover` on a portrait inside a
16:9 box keeps only the middle third of the height, which decapitates most of
the full-body action shots.

So each frame has a `-wide.jpg` variant, cut from the original at 2000 px with
the crop position chosen per frame rather than centred. The first pass was
reviewed on contact sheets and eleven were re-cut: six had clipped a head, two
were too tight to read, and three had lost the equipment that was the point of
the shot (the sled, the kettlebells, the rowing handle).

Two known limits:

- **`camp-row-erg-front`** is shot face-on through the flywheel, so no 16:9
  crop shows a rowing stroke. The wide variant reads as an athlete mid-effort
  rather than as rowing. It is the weakest station photo in the set and worth
  reshooting from the side.
- **`race-start-pen-pair`** works as atmosphere rather than portraiture; the
  crop catches caps and jawlines rather than full faces.

Use the portrait original wherever the slot is tall or square. The coach tile
is `aspect-[5/4]` on mobile and `aspect-[4/5]` from `sm` up, so it takes the
portrait file directly.

The Chicago crops also drop the "HYROX CHICAGO" watermark, which sat in the
bottom-right of the portrait frames.

## 8. Rules note

`docs/strategy/rules/HARD-RULES.md` rule 4 says never use HYROX brand assets
and never imply official affiliation. Kieron cleared this set for use on
30 July 2026. The rule still binds the **copy** around the photos: HYROX
branding appearing in the background of a photograph of Ben competing is
descriptive, but nothing on the page should be worded so that it reads as
endorsement, and the "not affiliated with Hyrox" line stays in the footer.
