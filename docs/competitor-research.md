## Price bands

Apps: free-£16/mo budget · £20-40 mid · £40-55 premium (HWPO).
One-off 12-week blocks: £55-149 — proven willingness to pay.
Online 1:1: ~£150-230/mo visible entry (UP), £250-500 hidden-price premium.

**The gap: £60-120/mo "coached app" territory is thinly populated.**
Suth's Online Coaching tier (~£129/mo) sits right at the top of that gap
with a genuine Elite 15 athlete attached — nobody in the table offers that
combination. And the Hub at £8.99/mo undercuts every paid app in the set.

## Funnel patterns observed

- Sub-£50 apps: trial-first self-serve, no human contact.
- £150+ coaching: application form → strategy call (RNT, Rox Lyfe) —
  EXCEPT Ultimate Performance, which converts premium via pure e-commerce
  with commitment-length discounts.
- Quiz funnels are NOT dominant in the HYROX niche — ours, ending in a
  personal call from an Elite 15 athlete, is differentiated on both counts.

## Tactics worth adopting (sourced in research)

1. Per-race SEO landing pages (Runna builds one per HYROX event) —
   matches our planned race-city pages; add per-event pages with dates.
2. Partner/referral codes that extend the trial — wire into the existing
   partner programme.
3. One-off 12-week race block at £79-99 (anchored higher) — captures the
   proven £55-149 one-off market without cannibalising subscriptions;
   include Hub trial inside it.
4. Founding pricing for the Hub Plus launch (RoxFit Ultra pattern).
5. Commitment-length discounts as an alternative close for Online
   Coaching when the call diary is full (UP pattern).
6. Free tools as SEO top-of-funnel — the plan-maker PDF outclasses the
   crowded generic calculators.
7. Scarcity honesty: "spaces limited" only when true (Rox Lyfe pattern,
   and our hard rules require it be real).
8. Ecosystem bundling later (Strava x Runna pattern) — partner-gym
   bundles are our version.
