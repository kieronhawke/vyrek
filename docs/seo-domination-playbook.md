# Suth Performance — Google SEO Domination Playbook

Prepared 29 July 2026. Synthesises the Semrush keyword database
(docs/strategy/data/), the SERP research (docs/hyrox-seo-competitor-
research.md), and the site teardowns into one sequenced attack plan.

Standing gate: the site is **noindex until Kieron clears it** (with
re-confirmation). Everything below compounds only after that flips —
which is a reason to flip it as soon as the brand is ready, because
rankings take months to mature and the clock starts at indexing.

---

## 1. The strategy in one paragraph

Win the queries nobody serious is contesting (coach-intent, couch-to-
hyrox, race cities), with pages that carry things no competitor has: a
named, verifiable Elite 15 athlete; genuinely personalised programming;
and real local/race data. Publish steadily (15-25 pages/month), never in
bulk, with every page funnelling to the quiz or the free consultation.
Build the "Ben Sutherland = HYROX coach" entity relentlessly so both
Google and AI search cite us as the source.

### Wave 2 — the flagship pillar (weeks 2-8)

**Own "couch to hyrox" outright.** The SERP is an Instagram Reel and
£39 PDFs. Ship: a definitive free guide (3,500+ words, videos,
progression benchmarks, downloadable weeks), the 30-day email course,
and a /couch-to-hyrox hub linking the beginner cluster. This is also
the beginner door of the funnel, in Kieron's soft, non-intimidating
tone.

### Wave 3 — race cities and events (weeks 4-12, then evergreen)

The biggest evidenced cluster: 50 keywords, ~82k combined volume, avg
KD 28. Pattern proven by Runna (per-event pages) and HAC (then
abandoned — their ranking pages 404).

- /hyrox/events/[city] enriched into true race hubs: dates, venue,
  course/roxzone notes, journey planning, taper timing, "training for
  {city}" CTA. Targets: London (18,100/26), Glasgow (6,600/17 +
  glasgow-2026 5,400/16), Birmingham, Dublin (4,400/23), Cardiff
  (1,600/16), Malaga (1,000/**9**), Madrid, Rome.
- Ticket-release and pre-race weeks are predictable traffic spikes:
  calendarise content refreshes 6 and 12 weeks before each race.
- Ben's own race reports after every race weekend (proof + freshness +
  entity, one asset).

### Wave 4 — free plans that out-do PureGym (weeks 6-16)

Long-form free plan pages rank above paid ones. Ship per level:
beginner 12-week, intermediate, sub-90, doubles — each 3,000+ words
with tables, videos, FAQs, downloadable PDF (email-gated), authored
"Benjamin Sutherland, HYROX Elite 15" with Person schema. Each page's
job: outrank the gym-chain content, then convert with personalisation
("this plan, built for YOUR race date, free" → plan maker).

### Wave 5 — the location engine (continuous, 20-30 pages/month)

Already live: 130 conversion pages. The engine that scales it:
1. Expand the location DB toward the ~1,100-town UK list; enrich with
   the pack's data model (gyms with actual station equipment, nearest
   races with journey times, parkruns, routes, one human "Ben's take").
2. Uniqueness validator before any batch ships (hard rule 3).
3. Priority order: the 62 evidenced Semrush towns (all KD ≤15) →
   population × evidence. personal trainer manchester uk (1,000/15,
   $3.46 CPC) is the proof target.
4. One page type at a time: prove /personal-trainer/[town] and
   /hyrox-training/[town] rank before layering /hyrox-coach/[town].

### Wave 6 — commercial and comparison content (months 3-6)

- /hyrox-vs/[x] cluster + "best hyrox training app", "runna alternative
  for hyrox", "[competitor] review" — highest buying intent; we can be
  honest and still win because our differentiator is real.
- Gear cluster: hyrox trainers (2,900/22), hyrox bag (1,600/17), best
  trainers for hyrox (1,000/25) — affiliate revenue + commercial
  authority.
- Stations & weights informational: hyrox weights (1,900/18), sled
  push weight (1,900/25) — feed the station guides already live.

### Wave 7 — the moat, when unblocked

Results-data pages (times by division/age, percentiles, records,
venue analysis) target hyrox results (14,800/19, top CPC) and make
every location and race page unique beyond copying. BLOCKED on the
data-source decision; claim-your-profile route can start now.

## 3. Entity and E-E-A-T programme (continuous)

The asset competitors can't copy is Ben. Make Google certain who he is:

- Person + Athlete schema sitewide (name, sameAs → Instagram, hyresult
  profile, podcast episodes, Rox Lyfe interview, YouTube appearances).
- /about and /press link every verified media appearance (dossier has
  the URLs); press page "coverage" section is no longer empty — real
  items exist now.
- Byline: every post authored "Benjamin Sutherland" with credential
  strap; his review noted on technique content.
- Get listed: coaches.hyrox.com profile; pursue HYROX Online Training
  Partner status (Rox Lyfe/Centr hold it; it confers a ranking page on
  hyrox.com plus the trust badge).
- AI-search (GEO): Q&A formatting + FAQ schema everywhere, quotable
  definitive statements, glossary pages, original data when the results
  layer lands. Goal: be the citation for "hyrox coach" questions in AI
  answers.

## 4. Technical programme

- Sitemaps segmented by type (locations / races / blog / core) for
  per-section indexing diagnosis in GSC.
- Schema per template: FAQPage (locations, hubs), HowTo (technique),
  SportsEvent (race pages), Person/Athlete (Ben), Course (plans),
  LocalBusiness only where genuinely applicable.
- Internal linking mesh (rule: every location page → nearest race hub +
  relevant plan + times/benchmark hub; every post → cluster hub + 2-3
  siblings; every page ends in a contextual CTA).
- Titles/descriptions generated from data, never one template string.
- Performance stays 90+; content pages statically generated.
- GSC weekly: manual actions, indexed-page trend. Falling index count =
  stop publishing, audit.

## 5. Velocity and safety rails

- 15-25 pages/month, never spikes; 300-400 quality pages in year one.
- Buyer-type filter on all keyword work (no jobs/courses intent).
- No page ships without unique data (validator).
- HYROX trademark care: descriptive use only, disclaimer in footer,
  never in a domain.
- Never fabricate proof; ratings markup only with real reviews.

## 6. Measurement

Weekly: GSC coverage + queries, consultation requests by source page.
Monthly: keyword movement on the priority list, indexed-page trend,
funnel conversion by entry door. The single KPI that matters early:
**consultation requests per week from organic**.
