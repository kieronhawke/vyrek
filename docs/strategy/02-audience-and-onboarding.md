# AUDIENCE & ONBOARDING

## THREE SEGMENTS, THREE FUNNELS

| Segment | Who | Product | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Beginner** | Never trained seriously, or returning after years out | App-led, self-serve, heavy education | Low monthly — volume |
| **B. Hyrox athlete** | Race booked or considering one | SUV Performance Hub — full data product | Mid monthly — core |
| **C. Private 1:1** | Wants Ben personally | Direct coaching, capacity-capped | Premium — anchor |

**Segment C's strategic job** is to make the subscription look like a bargain. Visible on
site, clearly limited ("6 spots"), mostly sold out.

**The bridge that matters most:** Beginner → Hyrox. Someone who joins to "get fit" and
books their first race six months later is the highest-value journey in the business. A
beginner's 90-day plan should end with *"you're now ready to consider your first race"*
and a link to the nearest event.

---

## RESEARCH BASIS

Drawn from teardowns of Runna, Noom, Fitbod and Ladder:

- **Long converts better than short.** Runna's quiz is famously detailed. Length signals
  seriousness and increases perceived personalisation. Every additional relevant question
  raises trial-start rate because the user is investing.
- **Open with the goal, directly.** Noom asks it on screen one.
- **Always include an escape option on screen one.** Noom offers "I haven't decided" —
  removes pressure at the point where drop-off is highest.
- **Deliver real value before the paywall.** Fitbod shows a three-month progress
  projection immediately before it. Highest-leverage moment in the flow.
- **Paywall at the end of the quiz.** Annual highlighted, savings tag, weekly cost
  breakdown, social proof stacked at the decision point.
- **Behavioural questions, not just physical ones.** Feels diagnostic, not administrative.

## FLOW — 28 SCREENS

### Phase 1 — Segmentation (1–3)

**1. "What brings you here?"**
- Getting fit for the first time (or after a long break)
- Training for a Hyrox race
- Already train, want to get faster
- Not sure yet — help me work it out  ← *escape hatch, routes to beginner branch*

**2.** Age band
**3.** Gender (with prefer-not-to-say)

### BRANCH A — BEGINNER (4–20)

Core insight: **the barrier is not information, it's fear.** Every screen reduces anxiety
and builds belief. Roughly twice the reassurance content of Branch B.

4. Main goal — Lose weight / Build strength / More energy / Feel confident / Get healthy for my family
5. Where you're at — Haven't exercised in years / Occasionally active / Fairly active, no structure / Was fit once
6. Tried before and it didn't stick? — Yes, several times / Once or twice / First real attempt
7. **INTERSTITIAL** — *"That's the most common answer we get. It's almost never a willpower problem — it's a programming problem."* **Critical screen. Beginner drop-off concentrates here.**
8. What's got in the way (multi) — No time / Didn't know what to do / Got bored / Gyms are intimidating / Injury / Lost motivation alone
9. Where will you train — Full gym / Home minimal kit / Home some kit / Outdoors / Mix
10. Equipment available *(conditional on 9)*
11. Days per week — 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+
12. Session length — 20 / 30 / 45 / 60+ min
13. Injuries or conditions — free text + common checkboxes
14. Sleep quality *(behavioural — feeds recovery framing)*
15. Current nutrition — 4 honest options, no judgement in the copy
16. Want nutrition guidance? — Yes / Not yet / Just training
17. Weight + height — **unit toggle mandatory: stones, kg, lbs**
18. Target — with *"I don't have a number, I just want to feel better"*
19. **MEET BEN** — photos, Elite 15, records. Beginner framing: *"Ben races at the elite level. He also coaches people who've never set foot in a gym — and he built this plan for them."*
20. **THE REVEAL** — predicted timeline to goal, what week 1 looks like, what week 12 looks like → paywall

---

### BRANCH B — HYROX (4–22)

Assumes knowledge. Should feel like talking to a coach who respects them.

4. Raced before? — Never / Once / 2–5 / 6+ / Competing for qualification
5. *(if raced)* **PB and location — pre-filled from the Results Hub.** **This is the magic moment of the entire funnel.** Pulling their real race result at screen 5 is unreplicable without our data layer.
6. Race booked? — Yes (date + city picker) / Looking / Not yet
7. Division — Singles / Doubles / Relay / Pro
8. Target — Finish / Sub-90 / Sub-80 / Sub-70 / Sub-60 / Qualify for Worlds
9. **INTERSTITIAL** — where that target sits in the real distribution: *"Sub-75 would put you in the top 18% of men 30–39 in the UK this season."*
10. Running background — 5k/10k/HM/M times, or "don't run much"
11. Strength background — key lifts or self-rated
12. Which stations worry you most (multi, all eight) — drives programming AND content recommendations
13. Sled push/pull experience — surface access is a real constraint
14. Equipment access (multi) — ski erg / rower / sled / wall ball / sandbag / farmers handles → drives substitution logic
15. Training days per week
16. Session length
17. Current weekly running volume
18. Strength training frequency
19. Injuries / limitations
20. Nutrition support?
21. **MEET BEN** — credentials-forward. Race record, Elite 15, splits, results.
22. **THE REVEAL** — predicted finish time benchmarked against real race data, projected improvement, station-by-station time targets → paywall

## MECHANICS

- **Email capture at ~screen 8.** Captures the 60%+ who won't finish. Abandonment
  sequence is usually the highest-ROI email in a quiz funnel.
- **Progress bar throughout.** Non-negotiable.
- **Back navigation stays.** Removing it in a 28-screen flow will hurt completion.
- **No auto-advance on multi-select.** Single-select only.
- **Paywall:** annual highlighted, savings badge, weekly cost breakdown, real testimonials
  only.
