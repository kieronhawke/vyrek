# HARD RULES — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Violating any of these damages the business. If a task appears to require breaking one,
stop and flag it to Kieron rather than proceeding.

---

## 1. NO FABRICATED SOCIAL PROOF

No invented testimonials. No fake press logos. No made-up statistics. No placeholder
counters presented as real ("2,847 athletes trained"). No illustrative reviews.

Fabricated proof was already stripped from this codebase once, pre-launch. Do not
reintroduce it in any form, including as temporary placeholder content.

If real proof does not exist yet, leave the space empty or write honest copy about what
the product does.

---

## 2. BUYER-TYPE FILTER ON ALL KEYWORD WORK

`data/keywords.csv` contains a `buyer_type` column. **Only build pages for
`buyer_type = Client`.**

You will find very easy keywords that are not customers:

| Keyword | Volume | KD | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| personal trainer jobs | 1,900 | 15 | Wants employment |
| personal fitness instructor jobs | 1,600 | 13 | Wants employment |
| level 3 personal trainer course online | 480 | 12 | Wants to *become* a PT |
| pt courses liverpool | 110 | 11 | Wants to *become* a PT |
| womens only gym near me | 590 | 14 | Wants a physical venue |

They are easy precisely because nobody selling coaching competes for them. Ranking for
them produces traffic that never converts.

**Exception:** the `PT Courses` cluster averages **$5.59 CPC** — the highest in the
database. It is a legitimate *affiliate* revenue line. It is not core content and must
not be built during Phases 1–6.

---

## 3. NO PAGE WITHOUT UNIQUE DATA

Every programmatic page must carry data that exists nowhere else in the page set.

If a page can be generated by swapping a place name into a paragraph, **it does not ship.**

The uniqueness validator (`rules/uniqueness-validator.md`) enforces this automatically.
Do not add a bypass flag. Do not disable it to hit a page count.

Google's "scaled content abuse" policy names this exact pattern. Sites caught by it saw
50–80% traffic drops, deindexing, and manual actions. Recovery takes months or never happens.

---

## 4. HYROX TRADEMARK

- Descriptive use is fine: "Hyrox training plan", "Hyrox results"
- **Never** imply official affiliation or partnership
- **Never** use their logo or brand assets
- **Never** register a domain containing "hyrox"
- Keep a clear "not affiliated with Hyrox" line in the site footer

Hyrox operates an active IP enforcement team.

---

## 5. WORKOUT-SAVE AND WATCH-SYNC ARE P0

These are the primary driver of one-star reviews for Runna. Treat them as critical from
sprint one, not as polish. A lost workout is a churned subscriber.

---

## 6. TRANSACTIONAL EMAIL STAYS OFF THE ROOT DOMAIN

All automated sending — trial welcomes, receipts, password resets, partner emails,
marketing — goes from `mail.suvathletic.com` with its own SPF and DKIM.

One spam-complaint spike on the root domain and Ben's personal replies start landing in junk.

Never create a `noreply@` address.

---

## 7. NO MACHINE-TRANSLATED PAGES

Auto-translation is explicitly named as scaled content abuse. When a language market is
worth entering, commission native content or do not enter it.

---

## 8. INCREMENTAL DEPLOYMENT

Preview first. Deploy in stages. Surface risks in specific items rather than shipping
batches wholesale — especially for quiz and onboarding changes, where conversion telemetry
does not yet exist to catch regressions.

---

## 9. QUIZ SPECIFICS

- **Keep back navigation.** In a 28-screen flow, removing it will hurt completion.
- **No auto-advance on multi-select.** Single-select only.
- **Progress bar throughout.** Non-negotiable.
