# BRAND & IDENTITY

## NAME

**SUV Athletic**

Derived from Benjamin Sutherland's real nickname ("Ben-suv", "Suv"). The vehicle
association is deliberate and load-bearing, not accidental: **all-terrain, built to carry
load, nothing it can't handle** — which is the literal definition of a hybrid athlete and
exactly what Hyrox tests.

Selected over: All-Terrain Athlete (hides Ben, whose credentials are the moat), SUTH
(homophone for "South" — traffic loss), Coach Benny (prices the brand down), Overland
(no connection to Ben).

**Do not use bare initials.** Benjamin Sutherland = BS. Never `BS Performance`,
`BS Coaching`, or any variant. Use SUTH or BJS if initials are ever needed.

**Avoid the generic "Sutherland + Fitness/Strength/Performance" pattern** — Sutherland
Shire in Sydney is saturated with gyms and dominates that search space.

## TAGLINE

*All-terrain athlete. Built by Benjamin Sutherland.*

Lock this early so the vehicle reference reads as deliberate rather than accidental.

## POSITIONING

Ben's name and race credentials sit **directly under the wordmark on the homepage**.
The brand carries the personality; his name carries the proof. Never hide him — his
Elite 15 status is the entire moat.

near-black.

Add `support@` only when volume genuinely demands it. Add `billing@` at VAT registration.
**Never create `noreply@`.**

The product is "an Elite 15 athlete coaches you personally." A reply from a ticketing
queue contradicts that on the first touchpoint. Keep the *from* name human throughout —
"Ben from SUV Athletic" outperforms "SUV Athletic".

## TONE

Direct, warm, credible. Beginner-facing copy reduces anxiety; athlete-facing copy
respects expertise. No hype, no fabricated urgency, no invented proof.
