# SUV PERFORMANCE HUB — PRODUCT SPEC

Positioned as **"Apple Fitness+ for Hyrox."** App-feel on web first, native iOS/Android later.

---

## TIER 1 — FREE (the SEO engine)

Exists to rank and to capture email.

- Results lookup and athlete search
- Basic race analytics
- City and event pages
- Benchmark calculators
- **Public leaderboard** — the habit-forming hook

## TIER 2 — SUBSCRIPTION (the business)

- Adaptive training plans — Hyrox and general fitness
- Full video library — every station, every fault, every regression
- **Workout logging with watch sync**
- Full analytics: split projections, station benchmarking, percentile ranking against
  real field data
- **Competitor tracking** — follow rivals, get alerted when they post a time
- Race-day pacing plans built from actual venue data
- Food logging
- Community

## TIER 3 — PRIVATE COACHING WITH BEN

Capacity-capped, application-based, premium. The price anchor that makes Tier 2 look like
a bargain.

---

## P0 — TREAT AS CRITICAL FROM SPRINT ONE

**Workout-save reliability** and **watch sync**.

These are the primary driver of one-star reviews for Runna. There is no reason to repeat
that mistake. A lost workout is a churned subscriber.

---

## DEFERRED — DO NOT BUILD IN V1

**Race Day Mode.** Intentionally deferred to avoid launching an unfinished signature
feature. Planned to coincide with a major Hyrox event for organic coverage. This decision
is final — do not scope it in.

**Nutrition features** beyond basic food logging. Post-V1.

---

## THE UNREPLICABLE MOMENT

Quiz screen 5 (Hyrox branch) pulls the user's **real PB from the results database** and
pre-fills it.

No competitor can copy this without the data layer. It is the single most impressive
moment in the funnel and the clearest demonstration of why this product is different.
Prioritise it in the Results Hub build.
