## 1. CORRECTION: £80 IS NOT UNDERPRICED

In doc 12 I said £80 for personal programming looked low, and benchmarked it against full
human coaching at £150–300. **That was the wrong comparison and the conclusion was wrong.**

The UK market splits programming and coaching into two distinct price bands:

> Structured HYROX-specific programming: **£30–£80 per month.**
> Coaching: **£100–£250 per month.**

So:

| Your tier | Your price | Market band | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Programming | £80 | £30–80 | **Top of band** |
| 1:1 Coaching | £120–250 | £100–250 | **Mid-to-top of band** |

Your pricing is accurate. Programming at £80 is at the ceiling of what programming commands,
and £120–250 sits properly inside the coaching band. Nothing needs changing.

*(Note: you said £100–150 in one breath and £120–250 in the tier list. Assuming £120–250 is
the considered figure — see §6 for a proposed structure inside that range.)*

### The problem this creates

**ROXFIT already has the race results database, it has live leaderboards, it has watch sync,
and it is free.**

Doc 03 built the entire SEO and product strategy on the Results Hub being the moat. That
claim is now materially weaker. Someone has a 3-million-race database and gives it away.

**This needs saying clearly rather than glossed over.** The Results Hub is still necessary —
you cannot be credible in this category without it, and it still powers the location pages
and the SEO architecture, which remains sound. But it is a **hygiene factor, not a moat.**
It gets you to the table. It doesn't win.

### What the actual moat is

Look down the competitor list again. Not one of them has a **named Elite 15 athlete who
personally builds your programme and answers your messages.**

Centr comes closest — but "the methods of former World Champions" is a licensed methodology,
not a person you can message on a Tuesday. ROXFIT has data and an AI assistant. FORMD has
diagnostics. Edge has onboarding. Runna has the best algorithm in the business and no human
at all.

**Ben is the moat.** Every tier should be designed as a rung on a ladder toward more of him.
That reframe should propagate through the whole product: the data layer earns the traffic,
the human converts it and retains it.

## 6. TIER 3 — 1:1 COACHING

Currently undefined, which is blocking its launch. Here's a proposed structure inside your
£120–250 range.

**What justifies 2–3× the programming price is his eye and his time.** The single strongest
differentiator available:

### Video form review
Client uploads a clip of their sled push, their wall balls, their lunges. Ben watches it and
sends back voice-note feedback.

This genuinely cannot be automated, cannot be templated, and is the thing that most improves
a mid-pack athlete's time. No app on the list offers it. It should be the headline feature of
Tier 3.

### Proposed structure

| | **Coaching £150/mo** | **Elite £250/mo** |
|---|---|---|
| Everything in Programming | ✅ | ✅ |
| Contact | **Weekly** check-in | Weekly video call |
| Response time | 24h | Same day |
| **Video form review** | 2/month | Unlimited |
| Programme adjustment | Weekly | Continuous |
| Race support | Pacing plan + race-week call | Full race-day support + live check-ins |
| Post-race debrief | ✅ | ✅ + full split analysis |
| Capacity | 15 | **6** |

**£120 as a founding-client rate** for the first cohort, grandfathered permanently. Gives you
a reason to close now and a story later.

### Make capacity visible
"6 Elite spots — 2 remaining." Real scarcity, honestly stated. It's the truthful version of
urgency and it's the strongest conversion mechanic available to a capacity-limited service.

## 7. THE £12.99 → £80 GAP

Six times. Wide enough that people who want more than Hub but can't justify £80 fall through.

**Option: a middle tier at £34.99.**

Templated plan matched to your race and equipment (not bespoke) · monthly **group** coaching
call with Ben · one video form review per month · priority in the weekly Q&A.

**Argument for:** captures the gap, group calls scale, form review at low volume is cheap.
**Argument against:** it cannibalises £80, and four paid tiers is a lot of explaining.

**My recommendation: don't build it yet.** Launch three paid tiers, watch where people
hesitate on the pricing page, and add it only if there's evidence of a real gap. Adding a
tier later is easy; removing one is not.

## 8. HOW WE'RE BETTER AND MORE AFFORDABLE — THE SUMMARY

You asked for both. Here's the honest version of each.

### More affordable
- Hub undercuts Runna (£12.99 vs £15.99) while being Hyrox-specific
- Hub annual (£129) costs less than one race entry
- Programming at £80 includes fortnightly calls that nothing else in the £30–80 band includes
- 1:1 sits mid-band while delivering an Elite 15 athlete, where the band is mostly filled by
  generic personal trainers

### Better
1. **A real named elite athlete, personally.** The one thing no competitor has.
2. **Video form review.** Cannot be automated. Nobody offers it.
3. **Race conflict resolution.** Multi-race periodisation, solved. Nobody does this at all.
4. **The Coach's Note.** Human reasoning on every block, mandatory, no AI-assist. In a market
   drowning in generated content, verifiable humanity is the product.
5. **Format freedom.** Excel, PDF, interactive, calendar — client's choice. Everyone else
   forces you into their app.
6. **Weekly Q&A at £12.99.** Real access to a real athlete at entry price.
7. **No fabricated social proof.** Every number and testimonial real. Increasingly rare and
   increasingly noticed.

### Where we're behind, and should be honest about it
- ROXFIT's leaderboards are mature and populated; ours start empty
- Runna's running algorithm is better than anything we'll build for running alone
- Centr has official Hyrox certification; we don't
- ROXFIT is free and on Android; our PWA needs to be excellent to compete

**None of those are lost on the strength of Ben.** But don't plan as though the data layer
alone wins, because it doesn't any more.
